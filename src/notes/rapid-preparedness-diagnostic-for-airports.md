A contingency plan on file does not show that it works with the staff on duty, at any hour, when a traveller falls ill. This diagnostic helps port health teams and their partners check the evidence for each step in one day, see which functions are in place and which are not, and decide what to fix first.

Two trained assessors review 31 items in six modules on site. Each item is scored 0, 1 or 2 against criteria written for that item, and every score must rest on recorded evidence. The method keeps three results separate: what the evidence shows is in place, how strong that evidence is, and how much of the review was completed. It ends with flags on seven critical functions and a prioritised list of actions, each with an owner and a test of when it is done.

It is not certification, IHR designation, a finding of compliance, an aviation audit, an external evaluation or a ranking of airports, and it does not replace the WHO assessment tool for designated points of entry. The methodology note contains no results, data or site information from any assessment.
