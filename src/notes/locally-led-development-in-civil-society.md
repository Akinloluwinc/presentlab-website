A co-design and prototyping toolkit created for the Local Leadership Labs: a practical resource for civil society organisations, community groups, intermediaries, funders and government actors advancing locally led development.

The Labs work through discovery, co-design and prototyping with civil society. The toolkit carries the same principle: design with, not for. Its tools are meant to be adapted to local realities, capacities and power dynamics, never applied as a fixed recipe.
