The toolkit is for civil society organisations, community groups, intermediaries, funders and government actors advancing locally led development.

The Local Leadership Labs work through discovery, co-design and prototyping with civil society, and the toolkit follows the same principle: design with, not for. Its tools are meant to be adapted to local realities, capacities and power dynamics.
