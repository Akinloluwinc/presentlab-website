Practice 02 — Health Security and Biosecurity

Commitments are easy. Capacity has to work on the day.

We support governments, border health authorities, security agencies and multilateral bodies to prepare for, detect and respond to health emergencies, with particular depth at airports, seaports and land crossings. We assess what is in place, help fix the gaps that matter most, and test that the fixes work.

What we do

Six lines of work

Commissioned separately or sequenced into a multi-year programme.

01

Preparedness assessment

Rapid and full assessments of points of entry and national capacities against the IHR (2005): scored findings, the evidence behind each score, and a prioritised action list.

02

Emergency response

Support to authorities during an outbreak: incident action plans for points of entry, operational protocols, job aids, traveller messaging and short readiness checks, working within the national incident structure.

03

Points-of-entry capacity

Cross-sector facilitation, training with supervised practice and competency checks, contingency plans, procedures at the point of use, simulation exercises, and ship sanitation certification.

04

Biosecurity and multisector coordination

Biorisk management and coordination between health, security and defence agencies, tested through tabletop and functional exercises.

05

Policy and institutional reform

Putting the amended IHR and the WHO Pandemic Agreement into practice: legal and governance instruments, service standards, and the institutions that deliver them.

06

Surveillance, cross-border cooperation and financing

Linking points of entry to national surveillance, information sharing between neighbouring countries, and costed programmes with clear ownership — including the financing, market decisions and last-mile logistics for medical countermeasures during public health emergencies.

One method

Evidence into action. Capability that lasts.

Five steps, in order. Each one produces something the institution keeps.

Understand

What is in place, what the evidence shows, and which gaps matter most.

Design

Fixes sized to the institution that has to run them.

Test

Exercises and readiness checks before anything is relied on.

Embed

Procedures, training and ownership inside the institution, not alongside it.

Review

Re-assess on a set date, and measure only what the evidence can support.

Work with us

Three ways in

Commission

Commission an assignment

An assessment, exercise, policy review, protocol or feasibility study, with an agreed question, scope and outputs. For points of entry we offer a facilitated rapid diagnostic, a four-to-six-week preparedness sprint on priority gaps, a remote readiness check during an outbreak, and follow-up verification.

Fund

Fund a programme

Programmes with defined priorities, institutional ownership and a plan for measuring change.

Deliver

Deliver with us

Joint proposals and delivery partnerships with clear roles and accountability.
